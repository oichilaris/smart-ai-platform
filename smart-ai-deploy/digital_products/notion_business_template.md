# NOTION TEMPLATE: Business Management System
## All-in-One Workspace for Entrepreneurs

### 📋 DASHBOARD (Home)
- Quick stats: Revenue, Expenses, Profit, Tasks Due
- Calendar view of upcoming deadlines
- Quick links to all sections
- Recent activity feed

### 💼 BUSINESS OVERVIEW
- Company profile & mission
- Team directory with roles
- Key metrics & KPIs tracker
- Quarterly goals with progress bars

### 📊 FINANCE TRACKER
- Income log (date, source, amount, category)
- Expense log (date, category, amount, receipt)
- Monthly P&L summary
- Budget vs actual comparison
- Cash flow forecast (3 months)
- Tax reserve calculator

### 📝 PROJECT MANAGEMENT
- Project board (Kanban: To Do, In Progress, Review, Done)
- Task list with priority, assignee, due date
- Sprint planning (weekly/bi-weekly)
- Milestone tracker
- Time tracking log

### 📧 CRM & SALES
- Lead tracker (name, source, status, value)
- Client database (contact, company, notes)
- Deal pipeline (stage, value, probability)
- Follow-up reminders
- Invoice tracker

### 📱 CONTENT CALENDAR
- Content ideas backlog
- Publishing schedule (platform, date, status)
- Performance tracker (views, engagement, conversions)
- Hashtag bank
- Content templates

### 📚 KNOWLEDGE BASE
- SOPs and processes
- Meeting notes
- Resource library
- Brand guidelines
- FAQ for team

### ⚙️ SETTINGS
- User preferences
- Notification settings
- Integration links
- Backup & export

---

## How to Use
1. Duplicate this template to your Notion workspace
2. Customize categories and fields to match your business
3. Start tracking from day 1
4. Review weekly in the Dashboard

## Tips for Maximum Productivity
- Set up recurring tasks for routine work
- Use filters and views to focus on what matters
- Link related pages for easy navigation
- Export data monthly for backup
